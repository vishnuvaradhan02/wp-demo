## Common issues and resolutions

**Camera keeps going offline**
1. Confirm the camera is within range of a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network (5 GHz is not supported).
2. Power-cycle the camera: unplug for 30 seconds, plug back in.
3. In the app, go to Device → Settings → Network → Re-pair.
4. Still failing after these steps → escalate to a human engineer.

**SmartLock battery drains fast**
- Expected battery life is 8–10 months with alkaline cells. Rechargeable 1.2 V cells drain faster and are not recommended.
- If it drains in under 2 months, the lock qualifies for a free warranty replacement.

**App login OTP not received**
1. Ask the customer to wait 60 seconds and use "Resend OTP".
2. Check the number registered in the app matches their phone.
3. If two attempts fail, escalate — support can issue a manual reset link.
